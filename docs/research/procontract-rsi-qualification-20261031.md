## Implementation and boundaries

- `scripts/procontract_operations.py` supplies immutable definitions, observations,
  knowledge/corrections, explicit assignments, durable reservation/dispatch,
  reconciliation, cancellation, pending input, selected waits and fenced adoption.
  It does not import the old RSI scheduler or its empirical gates.
- `scripts/procontract_operations_rpc.py` binds a capability socket to one admitted
  responsibility. Editing scope, offered measurements, actual authority and
  provenance remain host-owned. Task and research deployment kinds cannot be mixed.
- `strategies/research/operations.md` directs research through those operations.
  Saving knowledge does not select a version. Experiment choice and adoption
  rationale belong to the actor's context, not a required analyst/challenger sequence.
- Native action owners retain terminal receipts after caller cancellation.
  Publication outlives tool transport; inspection assessment and thread teardown
  await known local finalization. Unknown historical effects remain unknown.
  Confirmed reviewer/check interruption can return to a fresh strategy decision.
- Adoption uses the store's existing atomic Discharge + incumbent-selection
  transaction. Identical retries return their original receipt, including lost
  acknowledgements and later expiry; stale incumbents and changed requests fail.
  Original resources, owner epochs and cancellation remain distinct from outcomes.

E4 used the definitions at its recorded commit. Subsequent provenance checks added
complete package/tool-host/resolver fingerprints, observation of actual build
bytes, and a drift rejection before inference. Positive/negative fingerprint
controls are separate from the real episode; old definitions retain their narrower
historical meaning. Per-run delivered model payloads remain separate from definitions.

The audit also found E4's actor chose `wait {event:true}`: the earlier matcher did
not deliver its subsequent task event. The final implementation accepts/validates
boolean and named event conditions and reconstructs pending delivery from the
journal. A replay on a **copy** of E4 recovers the exact next-task outcome at cursor
14 while preserving acknowledged cursor 7 and owner epoch 0, with zero model calls.
The original E4 projection and events were not rewritten.
