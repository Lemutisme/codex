# Bounded test-time program learning: executable principles

This is a NEW protocol. Closed studies, old scores and unused authorizations are not
reopened. The installed interactive launcher is not claimed to implement it yet.

## What is being built

The native Codex executor and ProContract keep their existing task/check/review lane.
An opt-in host controller learns bounded execution (P), research (M), and runtime
steering (H) programs. The base model stays frozen. Call this **online evolutionary
policy optimization**, not weight-updating TTRL or a proof of general RSI benefit.
M produces the next program; an acknowledged experimental successor's M is used for
subsequent research. Method inheritance must be evidenced by actual request identities,
not the existence of a changed markdown file.

## Invariants and their observable tests

1. **Real feedback, not self-grades.** Native trajectory/status observations drive
   within-task proposals. Canonical fixed-denominator outcomes become rewards only
   after immutable submission. Missing/infra-invalid results remain unknown, not 0.
   Only numeric valid outcome receipts may update reward statistics.
2. **Learning != certification.** Permission to try one bounded candidate is fixed
   by the operator. A candidate can receive experimental execution without a claimed
   benefit. No independent confirmation means no stable/general promotion.
3. **Recursion is operational.** Every proposal binds parent P/M/H digests and the
   exact feedback packet. A later proposal must actually consume the inherited M.
   M reward measures descendant outcomes, not explanation quality; this is observed
   credit, not isolated causal attribution when P/M/H jointly change.
4. **Test-time means before action.** Research starts during the run, not solely
   after delivery. A successful expected-turn-fenced steering receipt must precede
   later executor activity to establish within-task uptake. ACK alone is not uptake.
5. **H must execute.** Candidate H runs in the existing bounded data-only interpreter;
   its actual facts, decision and dispatch are recorded. Unsupported operations,
   capabilities, unbounded predicates and malformed JSON are rejected.
6. **One budget.** Research's reserved deadline is deducted from executor capacity,
   even if concurrent. A shared wall deadline bounds the whole episode. At most one
   proposal and one steering attempt per episode; no retries on ambiguity/crash.
   Equal deadline capacity does NOT mean equal actual tokens or money: report both.
7. **No reward leakage.** The updater cannot see future instances, baseline outputs,
   hidden tests or another branch. Post-submission scalar feedback is permitted;
   exposure accumulates and cannot later be relabelled globally unseen.
8. **Host authority is fixed.** Candidate programs cannot edit checks, scoring,
   canonical pins, budgets, launch claims, permissions, credentials or adoption rules.
   ProContract support is local diligence, not canonical reward or independent gain.
9. **Isolation and cancellation.** Research has no execution environment or general
   filesystem tools. Evidence is bounded data. Stop/exit closes owned workers; no
   detached learning, silent model upgrades or concurrent project writers.
10. **Durable identity, no phantom inheritance.** Claim attempts before paid I/O;
    content-address programs; persist terminal outcomes. Unacknowledged steering,
    stale parents or unknown termination cannot publish an effective successor.
11. **Learning policy.** Maintain empirical reward observations for executed programs
    and descendant returns for their producer methods. Treat a newly generated program
    as an explicitly budgeted exploration arm. Preserve baseline and all rejected,
    malformed, inactive and unknown proposals. Never select the best hidden rerun.
12. **Claims follow evidence.** Separately report proposal, accepted dispatch,
    subsequent activity, recursive M consumption, valid task reward, comparison and
    all-in cost. Missing any stage is an incomplete loop, not automatic success.

## Small validation (pending a fresh budget decision)

Proposed: Luna/max, three prospectively ordered instances, frozen and adaptive arms,
one task attempt each (six total). Each arm/instance has 18,000 allocated seconds;
research is deducted, not added. Freeze identities/order before inference. Select
instances by environment readiness and declared order, not historical score.
Disclose prior operator exposure; never call this globally unseen.

The frozen arm has native ProContract too: compare learning, not contract vs no
contract. No automatic stable promotion. The adaptive lineage may use experimental
successors. Report paired scores and actual usage; N=3 is descriptive, not statistical
proof or a method-only ablation. Do not reopen a terminal study or append search.

## Review stages

C. New pinned six-run pilot and receipts, followed by claim-limited report.
D. Interactive integration is a separate product completion criterion, not inferred
